# Bloom Atlas
Data visualisation and an interactive tool for exploring when 3614 plant species flower and in what colour. Flowering months from NSW records — timing can shift in other states.

Search for species by Latin name to learn about their flowering months + flower colour instantly. Click a plant for a detail card (genus, family,establishment(native/naturalised), taxon distribution). You can compare up to 10 plants at once.

Paint: Choose months, pick colours. Click the centre to find matches.

## Source

Data derived from AusTraits (Falster, Gallagher et al (2021) AusTraits, a curated plant trait database for the Australian flora. Scientific Data 8: 254, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-021-01006-6)
